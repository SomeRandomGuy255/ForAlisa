For my Honeybun Alisa

One year & counting

My dearest Alisa,

First of all, I clearly owe you much apologies. Our very first anniversary deserved to be celebrated on the exact day, the day first truly loved each other. Im sorry that we didnt even get to celebrate our anniversary at all. Im sure that you had a lot of plans and that you wanted to do said plans, i dont know how much effort you put into them but im sure it would have been very lovely, and would have made us very happy. You deserve all that you can get, and we can do, on time and then some. But here we are, a little late and with a whole lot of love to make up for it.!

In just one year. It didn't feel like just a year, it felt like you've already become such a permanent, irreplaceable part of me and how i see everything. The way i moved through days, bad ones or good, is different because of you. Quieter, and warmer in the best ways, and all because of you.

Theres this ones song i like to keep coming back to I Wanna Be Yours by Arctic Monkeys. Every time it plays, I donut just hear music, I hear something that sounds like the future, and i imagine you in it. Our life life shared. Cozy mornings i wake up next to you, and big dreams that instead of random stuff, is just more time spent with you, and all the seemingly ordinary moments that become extraordinary because YOU, are there.

Thats what this year has been, and it's what i want more of, especially in the future.

Happy anniversary Honeybun! Thank you for being mine and mine only, and for letting me be yours.

With heart full of Love,
Vince
